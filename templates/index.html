🌍 Why SDG 8?

UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 calls for "full and productive employment and decent work for all." A key barrier to decent work for young people is the skills gap — the mismatch between what students learn in education and what employers actually need. This gap disproportionately affects students who lack access to career advisors, industry networks, or paid coaching.

AI CareerGap addresses this directly: it is free, requires no sign-up, and gives every student — regardless of location or background — the same quality of career preparation advice that others pay for. By making skill gap analysis and personalised learning plans accessible to all, we contribute to more equitable pathways to decent work and economic participation.
